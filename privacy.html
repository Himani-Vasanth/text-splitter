✂ SPLITTER — Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Splitter is designed to work locally in your browser.

Text Processing

Any text you enter is processed on your device. Your text is not uploaded, stored, or sent to a server.

Analytics

Splitter uses Google Analytics to understand overall site usage — things like page visits, approximate location, and device/browser type. Google Analytics does not receive the text you split; it only collects standard site-usage data, using cookies to do so.

You can read more in Google's Privacy Policy, or opt out of Google Analytics tracking in any site using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. Splitter works the same whether or not analytics is blocked.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser to distinguish visitors and sessions. We don't set any cookies of our own beyond what Analytics requires.
